Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This is a personal author website. It collects as little information as possible, and never sells or shares your data.

What this site collects

Analytics. This site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages readers visit, in aggregate. Google may set cookies and record information such as your approximate location, device, and browser. This data is used only to improve the site. See Google's own Privacy Policy for details.

Mailing list. If you choose to join the mailing list, the email address you provide is stored by the email service Brevo and used only to send occasional updates about new books and essays. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you may leave at any time. See Brevo's Privacy Policy.

Contact. If you send a message through the contact form, its contents are transmitted to the site owner so your message can be answered. It is not used for any other purpose.

What this site does not do

It does not sell, rent, or trade your information. It does not run advertising networks. It does not attempt to identify individual visitors from analytics data.

Your choices

You can decline or delete cookies in your browser settings, unsubscribe from the mailing list at any time, and request that any information you have shared be removed by using the contact page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date above.

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate, this site earns from qualifying purchases. Some links to books on this site are Amazon affiliate links; if you buy through them, the author may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It never affects the price you pay or what is recommended.
